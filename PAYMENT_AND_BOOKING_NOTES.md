The CBE and Abyssinia values displayed in the website are synthetic, **not valid payment destinations**:
- CBE: `0000000000000`
- Bank of Abyssinia: `0000000000001`

The telebirr display uses the existing site phone number `0986890425`. **It has not been verified as a telebirr receiving wallet.** Before taking customer payments, Joseph must verify the registered recipient name, wallet ownership, exact bank account numbers, and payment instructions. Remove the non-payment sample labels only after replacing them with confirmed values.

The payment UI copies numbers; it **does not initiate or verify transfers**. Consultation requests are not confirmed appointments. FormSubmit requires receiving inbox activation and live end-to-end testing. Facebook and TikTok URLs were supplied by the site owner and may require manual verification. Instagram and LinkedIn currently lead to platform homepages.
