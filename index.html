Off the clock, same energy.

Nutritionist and personal trainer with over 14 years of experience helping people improve their health, fitness, and quality of life. I specialize in working with people managing conditions such as diabetes, high blood pressure, and high cholesterol, as well as physically impaired individuals through adaptive exercise. I also coordinate exercise programs for children with special needs.

I combine exercise science, nutrition, and practical coaching to create safe, personalized programs, and I stay consistent with follow-ups, progress tracking, and accountability, so you're never left alone after the workout.

My passion for sport started at home. My father played professional football in the Ethiopian Premier League in the 1980s, and growing up around that discipline shaped how I coach today: not just to help people look better, but to help them move better, manage their health, and build lasting confidence.
