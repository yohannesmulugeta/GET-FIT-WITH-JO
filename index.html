01 / MEET JOSEPH ABRAHAM

The science behind the plan. The person behind the coaching.

I'm Joseph Abraham, a sport scientist, nutritionist and personal trainer. I believe good coaching should meet you where you are, not force you into a one-size-fits-all program.

My background combines exercise science and physiology, sports nutrition, chronic disease exercise support, adaptive exercise and programs for children with different abilities. Every plan is grounded in practical training and consistent follow-up.

My passion for sport started at home.

My father played professional football in the Ethiopian Premier League in the 1980s. Growing up around that dedication taught me that sport is about more than appearance—it’s about discipline, confidence, health and making progress that lasts.
