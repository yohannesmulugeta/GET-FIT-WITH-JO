01 / MEET JOSEPH ABRAHAM

The science behind the plan. The person behind the coaching.

I'm Joseph Abraham, a sport scientist, nutritionist and personal trainer. I believe good coaching should meet you where you are, not force you into a one-size-fits-all program.

My background combines exercise science and physiology, sports nutrition, chronic disease exercise support, adaptive exercise and programs for children with different abilities. Every plan is grounded in practical training and consistent follow-up.

My passion for sport started at home.

My father played professional football in the Ethiopian Premier League in the 1980s. Growing up around that dedication taught me that sport is about more than appearance—it’s about discipline, confidence, health and making progress that lasts.

BEFORE YOU START

Good questions. Clear next steps.

Whether you're getting started or returning to training, your plan should suit your goals and your life.

Ask Joseph your question →
Can I start if I'm a beginner?+

Yes. Joseph offers personal training for different starting points. Reach out with your experience, goals and available time so he can discuss a suitable approach.

Do I have to live in Addis Ababa?+

No. In-person coaching is offered in Addis Ababa, and online coaching is available for people outside the city. Confirm the practical arrangements with Joseph directly.

How much does coaching cost?+

Fees depend on the service and training arrangement. Ask Joseph for his current rates and available packages before committing.

Where do in-person sessions take place?+

Joseph works in Addis Ababa. The meeting location and schedule should be agreed with him before your first session; no fixed studio address has been confirmed on this website.

Can I ask about nutrition or a medical condition?+

Joseph offers sport nutrition coaching and exercise support for different needs. This is not a medical treatment service. For medical conditions, continue to follow advice from your qualified healthcare professional.

How do I book and pay?+

Send a consultation request with your preferred date and time. Joseph will confirm availability, rates and payment details directly. Never send money to an unconfirmed account.
